About

The art of seeing
differently

I am HemanthGaru — a visual storyteller based in India, with a decade of experience translating ephemeral moments into timeless imagery. My work spans celebrity portraiture, wedding, and intimate documentary photography, always guided by an obsessive attention to natural light and authentic emotion.

Every frame I capture is a quiet conversation between subject and space — a dialogue I am privileged to translate into image.
